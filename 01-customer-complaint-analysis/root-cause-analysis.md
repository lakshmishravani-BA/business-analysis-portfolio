# Root Cause Analysis

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this analysis is to identify recurring patterns in customer complaints and determine the potential operational root causes associated with those complaints.

The analysis uses a synthetic dataset created specifically for this Business Analyst portfolio project.

## 2. Analysis Objective

The analysis aims to:

* Identify the most common complaint categories.
* Identify recurring operational root causes.
* Examine the relationship between complaint categories and root causes.
* Compare resolution times across different complaint types.
* Identify complaints associated with repeat customer contact.
* Highlight potential areas for process improvement.

## 3. Root-Cause Analysis Approach

The analysis follows a structured approach:

**Complaint Pattern → Evidence → Potential Root Cause → Business Impact → Improvement Opportunity**

The analysis does not assume that a recorded root cause is a confirmed organizational finding. Root causes in this synthetic dataset represent suspected operational causes for portfolio analysis.

## 5. Root-Cause Analysis Framework

### Delivery Delay

**Observed Issue:** Customers may experience delays in receiving their orders.

**Potential Root Cause:** Delivery Delay.

**Potential Process Area:** Order fulfilment, shipment coordination, or delivery tracking.

**Potential Improvement Opportunity:** Review delivery workflow, tracking information, and escalation procedures for delayed orders.

### Incorrect Invoice

**Observed Issue:** Customers receive invoices containing incorrect billing information.

**Potential Root Cause:** Incorrect Invoice.

**Potential Process Area:** Billing data entry, invoice generation, or validation.

**Potential Improvement Opportunity:** Introduce additional invoice validation checks before invoices are issued.

### Product Defect

**Observed Issue:** Customers report problems with product quality.

**Potential Root Cause:** Product Defect.

**Potential Process Area:** Product quality control, inspection, or supplier management.

**Potential Improvement Opportunity:** Review quality-control checkpoints and defect reporting procedures.

### Refund Processing Delay

**Observed Issue:** Customers experience delays in receiving refunds.

**Potential Root Cause:** Refund Processing Delay.

**Potential Process Area:** Refund approval, processing, or handoff between teams.

**Potential Improvement Opportunity:** Review the refund workflow and establish clear processing timelines and escalation rules.

### Account Information Issue

**Observed Issue:** Customers experience problems related to account information.

**Potential Root Cause:** Account Information Issue.

**Potential Process Area:** Customer information management or account maintenance.

**Potential Improvement Opportunity:** Improve account-information validation and provide clearer procedures for updating customer records.

* Channel
* Priority
* Complaint_Date

These fields can be analyzed together to identify patterns and relationships.

## 7. Key Analysis Questions

The analysis should investigate:

1. Which root causes occur most frequently?
2. Which complaint categories are associated with each root cause?
3. Which root causes are associated with longer resolution times?
4. Which root causes have higher repeat-contact rates?
5. Are certain communication channels associated with particular complaint types?
6. Are particular complaint categories increasing over time?

## 8. Potential Business Impact

Recurring operational issues may contribute to:

* Increased customer contacts.
* Longer complaint resolution times.
* Additional workload for customer service teams.
* Lower customer satisfaction.
* Increased operational effort.
* Repeated processing of the same customer issue.

These impacts are potential implications for this simulated case study and are not measured actual business outcomes.

## 9. Root-Cause Validation

Before implementing a process change, potential root causes should be validated using additional evidence such as:

* Process documentation.
* Customer service records.
* Transaction data.
* Interviews with process owners.
* Workflow analysis.
* System logs.
* Quality-control records.

This prevents assumptions from being treated as confirmed root causes.

## 10. Expected Outcome

The root-cause analysis should help the organization identify recurring operational issues and prioritize areas for further investigation.

The findings will be used to support the next stage of the project: **process analysis and improvement recommendations**.

## 11. Important Note

This is a simulated Business Analyst portfolio project. The dataset, business scenario, root causes, potential impacts, and improvement opportunities are fictional and are intended to demonstrate Business Analysis techniques.
