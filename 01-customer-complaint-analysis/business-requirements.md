# Business Requirements Document

## 1. Project Overview

This document defines the business requirements for analyzing recurring customer complaints and identifying opportunities for process improvement.

The project uses a synthetic customer complaint dataset created for portfolio purposes.

## 2. Business Problem

The organization is receiving customer complaints across multiple communication channels and complaint categories.

Management needs better visibility into complaint patterns, resolution performance, repeat customer contacts, and potential operational root causes.

Without structured analysis, recurring issues may continue to affect customer experience and operational efficiency.

## 3. Business Objective

The objective of this analysis is to:

* Identify the most frequent complaint categories.
* Understand complaint volume by communication channel.
* Measure complaint resolution time.
* Identify the proportion of complaints involving repeat contact.
* Identify potential operational root causes.
* Highlight areas where process improvements may reduce recurring complaints.

## 4. Stakeholders

Potential stakeholders for this analysis include:

| Stakeholder                      | Interest in Analysis                                    |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Customer Service Manager         | Understand complaint patterns and service issues        |
| Operations Manager               | Identify process bottlenecks and operational problems   |
| Customer Service Team            | Understand recurring customer issues                    |
| Quality/Process Improvement Team | Identify improvement opportunities                      |
| Senior Management                | Monitor customer experience and operational performance |

## 5. Business Requirements

### BR-001 — Complaint Volume Analysis

The analysis shall identify the number of complaints by category.

### BR-002 — Channel Analysis

The analysis shall identify complaint volume by communication channel.

### BR-003 — Resolution Time Analysis

The analysis shall calculate and compare average resolution time across complaint categories.

### BR-004 — Repeat Contact Analysis

The analysis shall identify the percentage of complaints involving repeat customer contact.

### BR-005 — Root-Cause Analysis

The analysis shall identify the most common suspected root causes associated with customer complaints.

### BR-006 — Trend Analysis

The analysis shall examine complaint volume over time to identify potential monthly patterns or trends.

### BR-007 — Process Improvement Opportunities

The analysis shall identify potential process improvement opportunities based on complaint patterns and root causes.

## 6. Key Business Questions

The analysis should answer the following questions:

1. Which complaint category occurs most frequently?
2. Which communication channel receives the most complaints?
3. Which complaint categories have the longest average resolution time?
4. What percentage of complaints involve repeat contact?
5. Which root causes occur most frequently?
6. Are complaint volumes changing over time?
7. Which issues may represent opportunities for process improvement?

## 7. Scope

### In Scope

* Analysis of the provided synthetic complaint dataset.
* Complaint volume analysis.
* Channel analysis.
* Resolution-time analysis.
* Repeat-contact analysis.
* Root-cause analysis.
* Time-based complaint analysis.
* Identification of potential process improvement opportunities.

### Out of Scope

* Analysis of real customer data.
* Financial impact calculations.
* Direct implementation of process changes.
* Customer interviews or surveys.
* Production system changes.
* Measurement of actual business outcomes after implementation.

## 8. Assumptions

* Each complaint record represents one complaint case.
* Complaint IDs are unique.
* Resolution time is recorded in hours.
* Complaint categories and channels use consistent labels.
* Root causes represent suspected primary causes within the synthetic dataset.
* The dataset is sufficiently structured for exploratory analysis.

## 9. Success Measures

The analysis will be considered successful if it provides:

* Clear identification of major complaint categories.
* Measurable complaint patterns.
* Comparison of resolution performance.
* Identification of repeat-contact patterns.
* Identification of recurring root causes.
* Actionable process improvement recommendations.

## 10. Deliverables

The project will produce:

* Business requirements document.
* Data dictionary.
* Complaint dataset.
* Complaint analysis.
* Root-cause analysis.
* Process analysis.
* Business recommendations.
* Test cases for validating analysis outputs.

## 11. Traceability

The business requirements will be used to guide the subsequent analysis and ensure that each major business question is addressed by the project deliverables.

The requirements will also support traceability between business needs, analysis activities, findings, recommendations, and validation activities.
