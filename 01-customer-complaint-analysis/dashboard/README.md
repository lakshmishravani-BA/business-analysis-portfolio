# Customer Complaint Dashboard

## Purpose

This dashboard provides a visual summary of customer complaints to help identify complaint trends, recurring issues, resolution performance, and areas for process improvement.

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard will track:

* Total complaints
* Average resolution time
* High-priority complaints
* Repeat-contact rate
* Resolution status
* Complaints by category
* Complaints by channel
* Root causes of complaints

## Dashboard Sections

### 1. Complaint Overview

Summary of total complaints and key complaint metrics.

### 2. Complaint Trends

Analysis of complaint volume and patterns over time.

### 3. Complaint Categories

Comparison of complaints across different categories such as Delivery, Billing, and Product Quality.

### 4. Resolution Performance

Analysis of resolution time, resolution status, and repeat contacts.

### 5. Root Cause Analysis

Identification of the most common underlying causes of customer complaints.

## Business Value

The dashboard is designed to help business stakeholders quickly identify recurring customer issues, monitor service performance, and prioritize opportunities for process improvement.
