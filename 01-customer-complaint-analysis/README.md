# Customer Complaint Root-Cause Analysis

## Project Overview

This Business Analyst portfolio project analyzes customer complaints to identify recurring patterns, evaluate complaint-resolution performance, investigate potential root causes, and recommend practical process improvements.

The project uses a simulated dataset to demonstrate business analysis, data interpretation, root-cause analysis, and process improvement.

- **Project Type:** Business Analysis, Data Analysis, Process Improvement
- **Industry:** Customer Service / Retail Operations
- **Tool:** Microsoft Excel
- **Dataset:** 50 synthetic customer complaint records

## Business Problem

A fictional company is receiving customer complaints across multiple categories. Management wants to understand the main sources of complaints, assess resolution performance, and identify opportunities to reduce repeat customer contact.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze complaint patterns and category distribution.
- Evaluate complaint-resolution performance.
- Calculate key customer service performance indicators.
- Investigate repeat customer contacts.
- Identify potential operational bottlenecks.
- Recommend evidence-based process improvements.
- Present findings through structured documentation and an Excel dashboard.

## Key Findings

- **Delivery:** 15 complaints, representing 30% of the dataset.
- **Billing:** 10 complaints, representing 20%.
- **Product Quality:** 10 complaints, representing 20%.
- **Repeat Contacts:** 26 complaints, representing a 52% repeat-contact rate.
- **Average Resolution Time:** 46.4 hours.
- **Recorded Resolution Rate:** 100%, based on the status values in the simulated dataset.

Delivery is the largest complaint category. Repeat customer contact is another important area to investigate because it may indicate unresolved concerns, communication gaps, or opportunities to improve first-contact resolution.

A recorded resolution does not necessarily mean the customer was satisfied or that the issue was resolved on the first contact.

## Analysis Approach

The project follows a structured business analysis workflow:

1. Define the business problem and objectives.
2. Review the dataset and data dictionary.
3. Examine complaint categories and performance indicators.
4. Identify recurring patterns and trends.
5. Investigate potential root causes.
6. Review the complaint-handling process.
7. Develop practical recommendations.
8. Communicate findings through documentation and an Excel dashboard.

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Investigate Delivery Complaints

Review delivery delays, order fulfilment, tracking accuracy, and customer communication to identify potential causes of delivery-related complaints.

### 2. Reduce Repeat Customer Contacts

Investigate why customers contact support repeatedly and identify opportunities to improve issue resolution and communication.

### 3. Review Billing and Product Quality

Examine recurring billing and product-quality complaints to identify possible process issues and quality-control improvements.

### 4. Monitor Resolution Time

Track resolution time by complaint category and priority. Compare performance against defined service-level targets.

### 5. Improve Performance Reporting

Monitor complaint volume, resolution rate, repeat contacts, resolution time, and customer satisfaction to support ongoing process improvement.

## Project Limitations

This is a simulated business case study created for portfolio purposes. The dataset and company scenario are fictional and do not represent verified results from a real company or client engagement.

Potential root causes and recommendations would need to be validated using additional evidence before implementation.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how a Business Analyst can use customer complaint data to identify patterns, assess operational performance, and recommend process improvements.

The analysis provides a structured foundation for investigating delivery issues, reducing repeat customer contact, and improving complaint-handling processes.
