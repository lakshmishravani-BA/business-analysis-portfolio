# Customer Complaint Root-Cause Analysis

## Project Overview

This portfolio project demonstrates how a Business Analyst can analyze recurring customer complaints, identify potential root causes, and recommend practical process improvements.

This is a **simulated business case study** created for portfolio purposes. The dataset and business scenario are fictional and are not presented as real client work.

## Business Problem

A company is receiving recurring customer complaints through multiple channels.

Management wants to understand:

* Which complaint categories occur most frequently?
* Which channels generate the most complaints?
* Which issues take longer to resolve?
* How often do customers contact the company more than once?
* What potential process problems may be contributing to recurring complaints?

## Business Objective

Analyze the complaint data to:

1. Identify major complaint patterns.
2. Measure operational indicators such as complaint volume and resolution time.
3. Identify possible root causes.
4. Identify opportunities for process improvement.
5. Recommend practical actions that could improve the customer experience.

## Analysis Approach

**Complaint Data → Data Cleaning → Exploratory Analysis → Pattern Identification → Root-Cause Analysis → Recommendations**

## Key Business Questions

The analysis will examine:

* Complaint volume by category
* Complaint volume by communication channel
* Average resolution time
* Repeat-contact rate
* Complaint trends
* Relationship between complaint type and resolution time
* Potential process bottlenecks

## Deliverables

This project will include:

* Synthetic complaint dataset
* Data analysis
* Root-cause analysis
* Process analysis
* Business recommendations
* Proposed improvement measures

## Important Note

The data and business scenario used in this project are simulated for portfolio purposes.

Any findings, recommendations, or potential business impacts are illustrative and should not be interpreted as actual results achieved for a company.
